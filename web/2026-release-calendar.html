When will the 2026 Census results come out?

Statistics Canada has scheduled the first population and dwelling counts for February 10, 2027. Different topics arrive throughout the year.

Published release schedule

February 10, 2027
Population and dwelling counts
May 5
Age, gender, sex at birth and dwelling types
July 14
Families, households, marital status and income
September 8
Language, housing and Canadian military experience
September 28
Indigenous Peoples, mobility and poverty
October 27
Immigration, citizenship, ethnocultural and religious diversity, sexual orientation and general health
December 1
Employment, commuting, education, language of work and homelessness

These dates follow the schedule reviewed November 28, 2026. Some accompanying data products appear at a subsequent release. Check the current Statistics Canada release plan.

Looking for local figures today?

Compare two communities in the 2021 Census or follow the annual population estimates for children and seniors. The latter describe July 1 of each year and use 2021 metropolitan boundaries.
